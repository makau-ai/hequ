Assumptions

  • |v| < c (subluminal relative motion)
  • Both frames are inertial
  • Spacetime is Minkowski (no gravitational field)
  • v and c are measured in a consistent frame-invariant scheme

Derivation

  • Einstein, Ann. der Physik 17 (1905), 891 (on the electrodynamics of moving bodies)
  • Derived from the invariance of the spacetime interval ds² = c²dt² − dx² − dy² − dz²
  • Reduces to γ → 1 in the non-relativistic limit v/c → 0, recovering Newtonian mechanics
